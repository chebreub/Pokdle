## Movement

Active October 15–November 1, Paris calendar. First appearance after 1.4 seconds.
After a visit: 3.5–7 seconds of quiet on desktop, 4.5–8 seconds on mobile.
The first visit crosses the entire viewport. Three of every five scenes are moving traversals,
including lateral, diagonal and upward movement. Directions alternate; consecutive flights use different height bands.
Resting cameos choose among all available free positions and avoid their preceding position.
If a resting spot cannot fit, a full flight replaces it, rather than silently skipping the visitor.
Visitors are independent of game answers and can travel on all pages. Only one at a time.
Clicks, taps, typing, keyboard actions, dialogs, hidden tabs and reduced motion stop movement.
Navigation discards old paths and recalculates the next one. Scrolling, scrollbar drags and scroll keys do not interrupt a visit.
All decorations are non-interactive and aria-hidden.
Setting: Affichage → Décorations de saison.
